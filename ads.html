The Problem

Your Ads Don't Have A Traffic Problem.
They Have A System Problem.

Every one of these is a symptom, not the disease.

What This Usually Looks Like
  • Spending money on ads without knowing what's actually working
  • Getting clicks but not enough customers
  • High cost per lead or cost per customer
  • Creative that doesn't hold attention
  • An offer that doesn't give people a reason to act now
  • Targeting that's too broad or built on guesses
  • No real tracking, so nobody knows which campaign paid for itself
  • Landing pages that don't match what the ad promised
  • Campaigns that get changed every time performance dips, instead of diagnosed
Why It Happens

Performance Depends On The Whole Chain, Not One Link

How We Helped Moelle Zavian Generate $80.7K In Revenue

The Challenge

Moelle Zavian was relying almost entirely on Instagram DMs to make sales — no paid advertising running, and no email marketing system in place to capture or follow up with anyone who didn't convert on the spot.

The Strategy

Build the two acquisition and retention layers that were missing: a paid ad creative and campaign system to bring in cold traffic beyond DMs, and a full email marketing system to capture and recover revenue that DMs alone were leaving behind.

The Execution

We built and launched ad creatives and paid campaigns, and built out Moelle Zavian's email marketing system in Klaviyo — welcome, abandoned cart, and retention flows running alongside the ad account.
